Run limits and options

Crawl each site proves a college by reading its own pages: it is listed only when the extractor actually reads staff from one of its pages, and it needs no AI model. AI judges each page uses the configured model instead, and is the one to pick for conditions about individual people. These are work limits, not promises of complete coverage. If you request more colleges than the maximum, the shortfall will be reported.
